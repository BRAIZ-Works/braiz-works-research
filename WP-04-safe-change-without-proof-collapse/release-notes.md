# Release notes — WP-04 v1.0.0

**Safe Change Without Proof Collapse** — Change is inevitable in agentic systems. The challenge is to change safely without either rebuilding every proof from scratch or carrying forward evidence that no longer applies. This paper presents a public-safe safe-change model based on predecessor preservation, impact mapping, localized proof invalidation, regression, successor qualification, and readmission.

Public-safe release candidate; publication remains separately gated.

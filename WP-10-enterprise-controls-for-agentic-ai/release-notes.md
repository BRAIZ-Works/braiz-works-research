# Release notes — WP-10 v1.0.0

**What Enterprises Should Demand From Agentic AI Systems** — Enterprise buyers should evaluate agentic AI systems on more than model capability and task automation. This paper presents an implementation-agnostic checklist of outcome-level controls that procurement, risk, engineering, security, and operations teams can require without demanding a vendor’s proprietary internal blueprint.
